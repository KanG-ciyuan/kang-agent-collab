# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We want collaboration in this project to be precise, respectful, and safe. Participants should:

- discuss ideas and evidence rather than attacking people;
- state uncertainty and missing evidence openly;
- respect privacy, permissions, authorship, and project boundaries;
- give actionable feedback with enough context to understand it;
- welcome corrections, translation improvements, and different levels of experience;
- avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, or publication of private material.

## Scope

This standard applies to repository discussions, issues, pull requests, reviews, and other spaces where someone represents this project.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer when possible. Do not include sensitive personal information in a public issue. The maintainer may edit or remove harmful content, reject contributions, or restrict participation when needed to protect the community and project.

Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, documented when appropriate, and avoid exposing the reporter or affected people.
